# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-24 (rev. 3)_

This policy explains what Swipix ("the App") does and does not do with
your data. The short version: everything happens on your device, and
nothing is collected, transmitted, or stored anywhere else.

## 1. No Data Collection

Swipix does not have a backend, does not require an account, and does
not use analytics or activity tracking. The one exception is Google
AdMob, used to show a single, non-personalized ad when you choose to
permanently empty your trash — see Section 6 for what that involves.
Outside of that one moment, nothing you do in the App — which photos
you view, keep, delete, or restore — is sent anywhere.

## 2. Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your device's photo library solely to let
you view, organize, and delete photos and videos you choose. This
access is used only on your device, in the moment you're using the
App. Photos are never uploaded, copied off-device, or shared with the
developer or any third party.

## 3. What's Stored, and Where

Some small preferences are stored locally on your device, using
standard on-device storage — never transmitted anywhere:

- Your selected language
- Filter settings for categories (e.g. brightness range, file size
  range, the month/year you last selected)
- Which photos are currently in the App's trash, awaiting your
  decision to permanently delete them (nothing is ever deleted
  automatically)
- Whether you've completed the onboarding flow
- A small technical cache of values derived from your photos (such as
  brightness and blur measurements) used to power categories like
  Blurred Photos and Duplicates, so the App doesn't have to
  re-analyze the same photo every time

Uninstalling the App removes all of this.

## 4. Permanent Deletion

When you choose to permanently delete a photo, the App uses your
device's own photo library APIs to do so. The developer has no
separate copy and cannot recover it once deleted.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any
data from anyone, regardless of age — consistent with Section 1, there
is nothing to collect in the first place.

## 6. Advertising and Future Changes

Swipix shows one ad, served by Google AdMob, when you choose to
permanently empty your trash. Ads are requested as non-personalized:
AdMob does not use your activity in the App, or an advertising
identifier, to build a profile or target ads to you. Showing an ad
still requires sending Google some standard technical data (such as
your IP address and general device information) needed to serve and
measure it — the same baseline data any ad SDK requires. Emptying your
trash never depends on an ad actually loading; if none is available,
deletion proceeds regardless.

Swipix has no payments currently. If that changes — for example, by
introducing subscriptions or one-time purchases — this policy will be
updated to describe what's involved, and the App will ask for any
consent required at that time.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated at any time, including to reflect new
features described in Section 6. The "Last updated" date above reflects
the most recent revision — continued use of the App after a change is
published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
